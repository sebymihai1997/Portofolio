   Hello! Ever since I was a kid, I wanted to understand how computers work and what makes them fail. As I grew older, I started installing Windows on my own and creating my own versions of Windows XP, which I tested in virtual machines. Online gaming was a passing activity, rather than a passion, as it didn't give me much satisfaction, so I decided to build my own server to play on with my friends. That was when I began to understand more of the story behind games and programs. Since then, I've built and upgraded my own computers, always looking for the best components.

   Curiosity is my drive. I am constantly looking to expand my expertise and stay ahead of emerging technologies. My fundamental asset is my ability to solve problems. My analytical mindset allows me to efficiently identify issues, while my curiosity guides me toward effective solutions. I am driven and persistent, ensuring that every challenge I take on is resolved successfully.

   In my free time, I closely follow tech-related content and read industry articles. Some of my go-to channels include SmarterEveryDay, Technology Connections, Veritasium, ElectroBOOM, Electronics Repair School, Linus Tech Tips, The 8-Bit Guy, and ZonaIT, alongside major technology events. Engaging with this content allows me to keep pace with the latest technological advancements, assess their practical utility, and deeply understand the mechanics behind how they function.
